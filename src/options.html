SpoilerGuard settings

SpoilerGuard hides videos you do not want to see yet. It covers the thumbnail and the title with a grey panel, and you can click Reveal on any cover to see what is underneath. Everything you change here saves by itself.

1. What should be hidden

A block list is one topic you want covered, for example a game you are playing or a celebrity you are tired of. Each grey box below is one block list. Anything you block by right clicking a video on YouTube lands in My blocks.

2. Ready made block lists

These are written and shared by other people, so you do not have to think of every word yourself. Adding one copies it into your own settings, where you can edit it freely.

3. Where on YouTube to hide things

Two of these are off on purpose. Search results stay visible because you typed the search yourself, and comments stay visible because most people do not want their comments greyed out. Turn either on if you want to be extra careful.

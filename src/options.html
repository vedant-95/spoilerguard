SpoilerGuard

My lists

A list is one thing you want hidden, like a game or a person. SpoilerGuard puts a grey panel over any video that matches, so you do not see it by mistake. Click Show me on the panel when you do want to look.

Community made lists

Other people already wrote these, so you do not have to think of every word. Pick one and it becomes your own list. You can change it any time.

Share one of your lists

If one of your lists works well, you can give it to everyone else. Pick it below and SpoilerGuard fills the form in for you, so you do not type anything twice.

Which list do you want to share?

This opens a short form with your list already filled in. Press the green button at the bottom of that form to send it.

SpoilerGuard

My lists

A list is one thing you want hidden, like a game or a person. SpoilerGuard puts a grey panel over any video that matches, so you do not see it by mistake. Click Show me on the panel when you do want to look.

Ready made lists

Other people already wrote these, so you do not have to think of every word. Pick one and it becomes your own list. You can change it any time.

Where to hide

Choose which parts of YouTube SpoilerGuard should cover. Search and comments start off, because you asked to see those. Turn them on if you want to be extra safe.
